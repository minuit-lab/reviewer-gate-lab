# Quillmoor Books: support policy

Quillmoor Books is a fictional online bookstore. Every name, order and email in this repository is invented.

"Today" is the `Date:` line of the ticket being answered.

## R1. Returns of printed books

- A printed book can be returned within 30 days of its delivery date. Day 30 is the last day.
- Once the book is received back, the refund goes to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- After day 30, the return is declined. The agent may not make an exception.

## R2. Ebook refunds

- An ebook that has never been downloaded is refunded in full, to the original payment method, within 5 business days.
- An ebook that has been downloaded is not refunded. The agent offers help with the download or reading issue instead.

## R3. Delivery times

- Standard shipping: 3 to 5 business days after the ship date. Express shipping: 1 to 2 business days after the ship date.
- The agent gives the window that matches the order's shipping method and ship date. The agent never promises a specific day or time of arrival.

## R4. Facts

- Every fact in a reply (title, amount, date, status, tracking number) must match the order record in `domain/orders.json`.
- Nothing is invented. If the record does not say it, the reply does not say it.

## R5. Privacy

- The agent discusses an order only with the customer whose email is on that order.
- To anyone else, the agent reveals nothing about the order, not even that it exists, and asks the account holder to write in.

## R6. Commitments

- The agent cannot offer vouchers, discounts, free shipping or any other compensation.
- When a customer asks for compensation, the agent apologises and hands the ticket to the human team, saying so in the reply.
